# Stocky – Talk to Your Sales Data

## 🚀 Overview

Running a growing e-commerce or marketplace operation is tough when you can’t see what’s selling, what’s stale, or when the next stock-out will hit. **Stocky** lets business owners ask natural questions—“What should I restock first?” or “Which products are dragging me down?”—and instantly get data-backed answers. No spreadsheets, no analysts, no hassle.

---

## ❗ The Problem We’re Solving

- **Data trapped across platforms**  
  Sales and inventory data live in multiple marketplaces, storefronts, and spreadsheets. Consolidation is tedious, manual, and easy to ignore.

- **No analyst on payroll**  
  Solo founders and small teams can’t afford full-time analysts, yet they still need deep insights.

- **Out-of-stock paralysis**  
  Stock-outs kill revenue and customer trust, especially when supplier lead times are unpredictable.

---

## 💡 Our Solution: Talk to Your Sales

Stocky connects directly to your sales and inventory systems, harmonizes the data, and exposes it through a conversational interface. Ask it anything—“What items should I discontinue?” or “What will run out in the next two weeks?”—and it responds with clear, actionable recommendations. No dashboards, no BI training required.

---

## 📈 Business Value Justification

1. **Revenue protection**  
   Preventing a single critical stock-out often pays for the solution many times over—customers can’t buy what isn’t available.

2. **Time savings**  
   Instead of exporting CSVs and building spreadsheets, founders get instant answers. That frees up hours each week to focus on sales, partnerships, and product improvements.

3. **Data-driven decisions for small teams**  
   No need to hire an analyst or learn a BI tool. Modern LLMs translate plain English into sophisticated analysis, putting enterprise-grade intelligence in the hands of small businesses.

4. **Scalable foundation**  
   The connector model means new sales channels and suppliers can be onboarded quickly. More sources = richer insights without increasing overhead.

5. **Risk mitigation**  
   Predictive restock alerts reduce rush-shipping and emergency procurement costs, while better demand forecasts inform smarter purchasing decisions.

---

## 🧠 How It Works (High-Level)

1. **Data Connectors** – Pull sales, inventory, and supplier data from marketplaces, POS systems, spreadsheets, etc.
2. **Central Knowledge Store** – Clean, unify, and store the data in a cloud DB (or your operational store of choice).
3. **Predictive Models** – Track consumption trends and supplier lead times to forecast stock-outs and restock windows.
4. **Conversational Interface** – Use your Azure OpenAI deployment to allow natural-language queries inside VS Code, Slack, or a web portal.
