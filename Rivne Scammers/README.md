## 💡 The Problem

E-commerce businesses face critical inventory challenges:

- 📉 **Stockouts** lose 30-40% of potential sales
- 📦 **Overstock** ties up 20-30% of working capital unnecessarily  
- 🤔 **Manual decisions** rely on gut feeling instead of data
- ⏰ **Reactive approach** catches problems too late
- 🔄 **Disconnected tools** require constant context switching

**The cost?** Millions in lost revenue and wasted capital.

---

## 🚀 Our Solution

An **AI-powered inventory assistant** that acts as your expert inventory manager, working 24/7 to:

### 🧠 Predict What You Need, When You Need It

Our ML engine analyzes:
- Sales velocity trends
- Seasonal patterns
- Stock-to-sales ratios
- Historical data (ARIMA forecasting)
- Market signals

**Result:** Know exactly when to reorder before you run out.

### 🎯 Never Repeat Mistakes

The system **learns from your decisions**:
- Dismiss a recommendation? AI remembers and won't suggest it again
- Generates **alternative strategies** instead of repeating
- Adapts to your business preferences over time
- Gets smarter with every interaction

**Example:**
### ⚡ Act Fast on Opportunities

**7 Types of Intelligent Recommendations:**

1. **🚨 Critical Stockouts** - Products running out in <7 days
2. **📈 Trending Products** - Identify winners early (50%+ velocity spike)
3. **🐌 Slow Movers** - Free up capital with smart promotions
4. **🌞 Seasonal Insights** - Prepare before demand hits
5. **📦 Overstock Alerts** - Prevent cash flow bottlenecks
6. **💰 Price Optimization** - Maximize revenue with strategic discounts
7. **🎯 Stock Optimization** - Balance inventory for optimal cash flow

### 🔗 Seamless Shopify Integration

- **One-click sync** - Connect your store in seconds
- **Real-time updates** - No manual data entry
- **Direct actions** - View products in Shopify admin directly
