Customer Management

Adding Customers

  1. Navigate to the Customers page using the navigation menu
  2. Click the 'Add Customer' button
  3. Fill in the customer details: Name (required), Email (required and must be unique), Phone (optional), Address (optional)
  4. Add any notes about the customer in the Notes field
  5. Click 'Create Customer' to save
  6. The new customer will appear in your customer list with 'active' status by default
Tips:
  • Email addresses must be unique - you cannot add two customers with the same email
  • You can update customer information later by clicking on the customer and selecting 'Edit'
  • Use the Notes field to record important information about the customer

Editing Customer Information

  1. Go to the Customers page
  2. Find the customer you want to edit
  3. Click on the customer to view their details
  4. Click the 'Edit' button
  5. Update any fields you need to change (name, email, phone, address, status, notes)
  6. Click 'Save Changes'
  7. The updated information will be reflected immediately

Tracking Customer Interactions

  1. Navigate to the specific customer's detail page
  2. Scroll down to the 'Customer Interactions' section
  3. Click 'Add Interaction'
  4. Select the interaction type (email, call, meeting, support ticket, etc.)
  5. Add detailed notes about what was discussed or what happened
  6. Enter who created this interaction record
  7. Click 'Save Interaction'
  8. The interaction will be added to the customer's history with a timestamp
Tips:
  • Use interactions to maintain a complete history of customer communications
  • Detailed notes help team members understand customer history
  • All interactions are timestamped automatically

Product Management

Adding Products

  1. Go to the Products page from the navigation menu
  2. Click the 'Add Product' button
  3. Enter the product name (required)
  4. Add a description to help identify the product
  5. Set the price (required)
  6. Enter the stock quantity (required)
  7. Select or enter a category for organization
  8. Upload product photos - you can upload multiple images
  9. The system can automatically remove backgrounds from your product images
  10. Add technical details if applicable
  11. If you use Shopify or WooCommerce, you can sync this product later
  12. Click 'Create Product' to save
Tips:
  • You can upload multiple photos for each product
  • The background removal feature helps create professional product photos
  • Stock quantities update automatically when orders are placed
  • Products with stock below 10 units are highlighted as 'low stock'

Managing Inventory

  1. Go to the Products page to view all inventory
  2. Check the 'Stock Quantity' column to see current levels
  3. Products with stock below 10 units are highlighted in red as 'Low Stock'
  4. To update stock: click on a product, click 'Edit', update the stock quantity, save
  5. Stock automatically decreases when orders are placed
  6. Monitor the dashboard for low stock alerts
Tips:
  • Set up regular inventory checks to maintain accurate stock levels
  • The dashboard shows low stock products for quick monitoring
  • Consider setting reorder points for frequently sold items

Order Management

Creating Orders

  1. Navigate to the Orders page
  2. Click 'Add Order'
  3. Select the customer from the dropdown menu
  4. Choose products to add to the order
  5. Set the quantity for each product
  6. The total will be calculated automatically
  7. Select a payment method (cash, credit card, debit card, mobile payment, or bank transfer)
  8. Add the shipping address if different from customer's default address
  9. Click 'Create Order'
  10. The order will be created with 'pending' status

Changing Order Status

  1. Go to the Orders page
  2. Click on the order you want to update
  3. Click 'Edit Order' or change status directly
  4. Select the new status from the dropdown
  5. Available statuses: pending, processing, shipped, delivered, completed, cancelled, refunded
  6. Click 'Save' to update the order
Tips:
  • Update status as the order progresses through fulfillment
  • Customers appreciate knowing their order status
  • Use 'completed' for successfully delivered orders

Invoice System

Generating Invoices

  1. Go to the Orders page
  2. Find the order you want to invoice
  3. Click the 'Generate Invoice' button next to the order
  4. The system will create a Polish VAT-compliant invoice
  5. The invoice includes all order details, customer information, and itemized products
  6. You can download the invoice as a PDF
  7. Invoice numbers are automatically generated and unique
Tips:
  • Invoices follow Polish VAT regulations
  • Each invoice has a unique invoice number
  • Invoices include issue date and supply date

Multi-Channel Integration

One Front integrates with Shopify and WooCommerce for seamless multi-channel selling:

Shopify Integration

  • Products can be synced to your Shopify store
  • Inventory levels synchronize across platforms
  • Product details and images sync automatically
  • Changes made in One Front update your Shopify store

WooCommerce Integration

  • Products can be synced to your WooCommerce store
  • Inventory management across both platforms
  • Centralized product management
  • Automatic inventory updates
Tips:
  • Keep product information consistent across all platforms
  • Sync regularly to avoid inventory discrepancies
  • Monitor inventory levels to prevent overselling
