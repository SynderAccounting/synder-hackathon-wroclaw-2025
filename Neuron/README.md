### : OneFront Your AI-Powered E-commerce Command Center

OneFront, also known as One Front, is a comprehensive e-commerce management platform designed to centralize and automate your online retail operations. By integrating with major platforms like Shopify, WooCommerce, Amazon, and eBay, OneFront eliminates the need for manual data entry and synchronization across multiple storefronts. At its core, the platform syncs sales, inventory, and customer data into a single dashboard, providing a unified view of your business.

#### Key Functionalities:

**Multi-Platform Integration and Management:**
*   **Seamless Syncing:** Connects with over 25 platforms, including major e-commerce sites and payment gateways like Stripe, PayPal, and Square.
*   **Centralized Control:** Manage products, orders, and inventory across all channels from one interface. Updates to product details or stock levels are automatically pushed to all connected stores.
*   **Automated Bookkeeping:** OneFront automates financial record-keeping by syncing sales data, fees, and expenses with accounting software like QuickBooks and Xero.

**AI-Powered Analytics and Business Intelligence:**
*   **Predictive Insights:** Utilizes pre-trained machine learning models (XGBoost) to deliver accurate sales and customer traffic forecasts for daily, weekly, and monthly horizons.
*   **Data-Driven Decisions:** Provides automated business intelligence and performance metrics to help you make informed decisions and optimize your strategies.
*   **Customer Intelligence:** Features a CRM, loyalty points system, and customer interaction tracking to enhance your understanding of customer behavior.

**Streamlined Operations:**
*   **Order and Inventory Management:** Offers a unified system for managing orders from multiple platforms, complete with built-in shipment tracking and inventory optimization recommendations.
*   **AI-Enhanced Product Management:** Simplifies product presentation with tools for automatic background removal from photos, image optimization, and support for 3D visualizations.
*   **Professional Invoicing:** Generates Polish VAT-compliant invoices with automated tax rate suggestions, which can be delivered via email.

**Technical Foundation:**
*   The platform is built on a backend stack that includes Flask, SQLite, XGBoost, and Scikit-learn, ensuring robust and reliable performance.
*   Its modular architecture allows for the addition of new machine learning models and custom integrations with other e-commerce platforms.

In essence, OneFront is engineered to reduce operational costs, save time on manual tasks, and provide the AI-driven insights necessary to accelerate business growth in a competitive e-commerce landscape.
